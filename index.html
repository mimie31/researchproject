The Food Security Scenario

A major vulnerability in securing Food Security (SFM) within Malaysia is post-harvest losses caused by cold chain failure during transport disruptions (e.g., reefer truck breakdown or port delays under tropical heat).

As temperature containment fails, microbial replication and biochemical degradation trigger a sigmoidal spoilage curve. By determining the First Derivative \(f'(x)\), we compute the instantaneous spoilage velocity (% spoilage/hour). This enables food security officers to establish the Critical Window of Rescue before commodities break down into catastrophic food waste.

Mathematical Spoilage Model

Cumulative Spoilage \(f(x)\) (%):
$$f(x) = \frac{100}{1 + \beta \cdot e^{-k \cdot \alpha \cdot x}}$$
Derivative \(f'(x)\) (Spoilage Velocity per Hour):
$$f'(x) = \frac{100 \cdot \beta \cdot (k \cdot \alpha) \cdot e^{-k \cdot \alpha \cdot x}}{(1 + \beta \cdot e^{-k \cdot \alpha \cdot x})^2}$$
x = Duration of thermal failure (Hours)
100% = Total load destruction
\(\beta\) = Initial load freshness constant
\(k\) = Food biological sensitivity index
\(\alpha\) = Ambient Temperature Stress factor
