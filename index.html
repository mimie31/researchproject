SFM: Food Security Program Focus

Applying Derivatives to Post-Harvest Management

Real-time Spoilage Rate Modeling in Malaysian Rice Silos

Data Reference Framework: Based on the National Food Security Policy 2030 (KPKM) and OpenDOSM Rice Production & Supply Balances reporting paradigms.

1. Bioscience Scenario

In tropical Malaysia, maintaining stored paddy quality is a significant barrier to boosting our national rice self-sufficiency level (SSL). This model tracks cumulative grain spoilage percentage $f(x)$ inside a 50-tonne metal silo over a 12-day period without controlled atmospheres. Spoilage accelerates as respiratory heat and moisture build up, creating hot-spots.

Food Security Impact: Finding the exact day where the rate of degradation peaks allows automated systems to switch on targeted aeration, preventing downstream food waste.

2. Mathematical Equations

Cumulative Loss Function:
f(x) = -0.015x³ + 0.27x² + 0.1x

$f(x)$ represents total percentage loss of inventory, where $x$ is storage time in days ($0 \le x \le 12$).

Instantaneous Rate Derivative:
f'(x) = -0.045x² + 0.54x + 0.1

$f'(x)$ gives the instantaneous rate of change (% loss/day). Calculating this derivative unlocks when deterioration speeds up or slows down.
